History of Archery

Archery is one of the oldest human skills, dating back about 70,000 years to Africa. Before the wheel was even invented, early humans were using bows to hunt and survive.

Over time, it changed the world:

  • Ancient Egypt: They were the first to use bows in major battles around 3500 BC.
  • The Mongols: Genghis Khan's army became famous for shooting accurately while riding horses at full speed.
  • The English Longbow: In the Middle Ages, these massive bows could hit targets from over 300 yards away!
